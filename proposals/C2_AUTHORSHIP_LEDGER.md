## Rule for adding another C2 author

A person should be moved from acknowledgement/source attribution to the C2 author list only if they make a substantive contribution to the C2 paper itself, such as one or more of:

- materially shaping the four-failure synthesis or its ecological interpretation;
- supplying a substantial conceptual argument that changes the Opinion, not merely owning a cited source result;
- drafting or substantively revising the proposal/manuscript;
- taking responsibility for a defined part of the final argument and approving the submitted version.

The following are **not sufficient by themselves**:

- being a supervisor or senior colleague;
- being an author of a dataset or prior paper reused as evidence;
- owning one source repository/result that C2 cites;
- having commits in a repository;
- giving routine comments that do not materially change the conceptual paper.

## Source-owner versus C2-author distinction

C2 cites and preserves ownership of component results:

- Boundary — identification geometry;
- CED — target licensing and failure architecture;
- MROD — mechanism-learning observation design and ecological worked examples;
- REC — record-entry loss and irreversibility;
- TNOA — semantic restraint and operating-semantics portability.

Source ownership does **not** automatically transfer to C2 authorship. Conversely, a collaborator who materially helps synthesize these components for C2 can qualify for C2 authorship even if they did not author all source components.
