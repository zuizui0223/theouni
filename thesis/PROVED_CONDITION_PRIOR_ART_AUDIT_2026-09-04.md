# Proved-condition prior-art audit — 2026-09-04

## Purpose

The ten-chapter spine is now theorem-centered. The next risk is different: a correct theorem can still look trivial or derivative if the dissertation claims novelty at the wrong layer.

This audit separates, for each chapter:

1. **classical / nearest-neighbour substrate** that must be acknowledged;
2. **what the chapter actually proves or recovers beyond the slogan**;
3. **what not to sell as novelty**;
4. **the strongest defensible contribution to foreground**;
5. **the remaining literature burden before citation-ready status**.

This is a novelty firewall, not a firstness certificate. Absence of a cited nearest neighbour here is not evidence of priority.

## Global conclusion

The safest dissertation-wide novelty position is **not** “we invented new mathematics for eight unrelated problems.”

The stronger position is:

> A family of ecological inference problems that are often discussed qualitatively is recast as responsibility-specific exact conditions, sharp boundaries, minimal repairs, or fail-closed empirical tests; the dissertation then proves that these conditions are typed and cannot be replaced by one unlabelled notion of scientific richness.

The source programmes differ in mathematical novelty risk. Chapters 1–4 and 8 sit close to well-developed classical literatures. Chapters 5–7 contain stronger custom finite constructions/minimality statements, but they still require explicit firewalls against automata minimization, cybernetics/state abstraction, bisimulation/lumpability, and generic path-dependence literatures.

## Chapter 0 / TU-1 — reuse after scientific compression

### Nearest classical substrate

The state-only criterion

`q_Q = f ∘ q_P`

is elementary quotient/factorization logic. The minimum auxiliary alphabet is also close in spirit to zero-error functional coding / side-information problems. These components should not be marketed as standalone mathematical inventions.

### What is genuinely doing work here

- world-level construction of a sufficient state;
- later revision after the raw distinctions may have been discarded;
- state-only recoding;
- exact minimum side information when recoding fails;
- average global refinement versus worst-case locally hidden revision burden.

The theorem `D_avg <= D_rev` plus the family with arbitrarily small average debt and arbitrary worst-case revision debt gives the programme a nontrivial scale-separation result, even though the coding substrate is elementary.

### Do not claim

- invention of quotient factorization;
- invention of side-information coding;
- a universal theory of scientific change;
- that Chapters 1–8 are corollaries of TU-1.

### Foreground

**Scientific forgetting under contract revision:** a representation can be fully adequate when stored yet become unrevisable when responsibilities change, and the missing revision information is exactly quantifiable on the declared finite carrier.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Audit sufficient-statistic revision, functional/zero-error coding with side information, experiment comparison, and model-version/revisability literatures before any priority language.

## Chapter 1 / Boundary — when another measurement changes identification

### Nearest classical substrate

Structural identifiability is classical. Bellman & Åström (1970) explicitly framed the question of how much internal system structure can be inferred from input-output observations and what experiments are needed for unique coupling identification. Rank-nullity and linear observation-space arguments are standard mathematical infrastructure.

Reference: Bellman, R. & Åström, K. J. (1970), *On structural identifiability*, Mathematical Biosciences 7:329–339, DOI `10.1016/0025-5564(70)90132-X`.

### What the chapter proves

For the declared exact log-linear observation class:

- compatible mechanism dimension is `k-rank(M)`;
- a new scalar observation reduces structural ambiguity **iff** its row lies outside the existing row span;
- useful scalar observations reduce the dimension by exactly one;
- `k-1-r` for a product endpoint plus independent channel anchors is a corollary.

### Do not claim

- new rank-nullity mathematics;
- invention of structural identifiability;
- that molecular/proximal measurements are generally weak;
- that more precise observations are useless statistically.

### Foreground

**Ecological mechanistic-evidence design:** biological proximity and identification strength are separated explicitly, with the rank condition translated into joint field-measurement and channel/calibration-anchor requirements plus fail-closed `not evaluable` outcomes.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Compare directly with structural identifiability, observability/experimental design, partial identification, and ecological mechanism-measurement literature. Novelty language should sit in the ecological evidentiary synthesis/design consequences, not the rank theorem itself.

## Chapter 2 / EGWE — what perfect precedence actually identifies

### Nearest classical substrate

The early-warning literature already documents false positives, selection conditional on transitions, and the need for discrimination rather than event-only visual precedence. Boettiger & Hastings (2012) explicitly diagnosed a prosecutor's-fallacy problem in early-warning analysis. Related work evaluates sensitivity/false alarms using ROC/AUC.

References:

- Boettiger, C. & Hastings, A. (2012), *Early warning signals and the prosecutor's fallacy*, Proceedings of the Royal Society B 279:4734–4739.
- Boettiger, C., Ross, N. & Hastings, A. (2013), *Early warning signals of stochastic switching*, Proceedings of the Royal Society B; uses ROC/AUC to distinguish switching from non-switching trajectories.

The binary-score identity `AUC=(sensitivity+specificity)/2` is standard ROC geometry.

### What the chapter proves and recovers

Under perfect binary event-conditioned precedence:

- sensitivity is exactly 1;
- specificity is unconstrained by the event-only statement;
- binary AUC is `(1+specificity)/2`, so the same perfect precedence can span chance-to-perfect discrimination;
- the locked inherited and fresh ensembles both attain the sharp chance endpoint: `35/35` with `48/48`, and `33/33` with `49/49`.

### Do not claim

- discovery that false positives exist;
- invention of ROC/AUC;
- a universal theorem for continuous time-dependent early-warning scores;
- that genetic diversity is generally non-predictive.

### Foreground

**A frozen replication that succeeds perfectly at its original event-conditioned estimand and simultaneously fails maximally at predictive discrimination once the missing denominator is restored.** The value is the exact separation of two scientific responsibilities under pre-frozen rules, not a new ROC identity.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Audit early-warning selection bias, prospective prediction, fixed-horizon discrimination, censoring and ecological forecasting literatures. The manuscript should explain precisely why its frozen full-denominator design is not merely another demonstration of known false positives.

## Chapter 3 / MROD — when adaptive recomputation is strictly valuable

### Nearest classical substrate

Information-based experimental design is classical. Lindley (1956) introduced a Shannon-information measure for experiments; modern Bayesian optimal experimental design commonly uses expected information gain / mutual information. Adaptive experiment selection and active learning are also well-developed; adaptive submodularity gives sufficient conditions under which adaptive greedy policies have approximation guarantees.

References:

- Lindley, D. V. (1956), *On a Measure of the Information Provided by an Experiment*, Annals of Mathematical Statistics 27:986–1005, DOI `10.1214/aoms/1177728069`.
- Golovin, D. & Krause, A. (2011), *Adaptive Submodularity: Theory and Applications in Active Learning and Stochastic Optimization*, JAIR 42:427–486.

The inequality `E[max U] >= max E[U]` and its equality characterization are elementary decision-theoretic facts once branch utilities are defined.

### What the chapter proves and validates

- adaptive second-step value is never below the strongest precommitted static second choice;
- strict gain occurs **iff** the positive-probability branches have no common optimal candidate;
- a four-world deterministic witness reaches 1.0 versus 0.5 bits and is minimal in the declared branch-switch class;
- G2 independently validates the full information-guided sequential method under truth-peek-free controlled mechanism ambiguity, nuisance candidates, fixed budgets, and zero hidden-truth exclusion.

### Do not claim

- invention of mutual-information design;
- universal optimality of adaptive greedy experiment selection;
- that the common-argmax theorem is a deep new result of decision theory;
- superiority over all Bayesian/active-learning designs.

### Foreground

**Mechanism-resolving ecological observation design with a fail-closed candidate contract and a matched controlled benchmark**, plus an exact condition showing when recomputation itself—not merely information ranking versus random order—has strict value.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Directly compare MROD to Bayesian optimal experimental design, model discrimination, adaptive design, active learning, value-of-information ecology, and simulator-based design. State clearly what object `S` represents and why the candidate-verification/fail-closed layer is scientifically nontrivial.

## Chapter 4 / eco-genetic-criticality — when one directional scalar state exists

### Nearest classical substrate

Real-valued utility/order representation has a long literature. Debreu's classical work and subsequent representation theorems study conditions under which orders or preorders admit real-valued utility representations. For a finite set already equipped with a coordinatewise order, the fact that one scalar can exactly preserve all coordinates only under strong comparability conditions is mathematically elementary relative to that literature.

References:

- Debreu, G. (1954), *Representation of a Preference Ordering by a Numerical Function*, in *Decision Processes*, pp. 159–166.
- Herden, G. & Levin, V. L. (2012), *Utility representation theorems for Debreu separable preorders*, Journal of Mathematical Economics 48:148–154, DOI `10.1016/j.jmateco.2012.02.005`.

### What the chapter proves and recovers

For finite target vectors oriented so higher is no worse:

- an exact directionally coherent sufficient scalar exists **iff** the distinct target vectors form a chain under product order;
- one crossing pair is an impossibility certificate;
- the locked H3 2-patch versus 16-patch states provide such a crossing;
- an independent result separates potential viability from realised occupancy across 1,037 prepared sources;
- a separate alignment witness shows coarse marginals can match while next transition differs.

### Do not claim

- invention of utility/order representation;
- that no scalar ecological health index can ever be useful;
- that all five named states are always pairwise distinct;
- a universal fragmentation law.

### Foreground

**The finite eco-genetic model itself violates the exact representability condition needed for one directionally coherent state score**, and it does so through a locked target crossing rather than a verbal assertion that multiple dimensions matter.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Audit multi-criteria ecological condition indices, Pareto/multiattribute utility, resilience-state indicators, eco-evolutionary state definitions and composite biodiversity indicators. The mathematical iff should be positioned as a clean diagnostic; the ecological crossing is the main substantive recovery.

## Chapter 5 / CCOC — open futures and exact interface memory

### Nearest classical substrate

Future-equivalence and minimal-state ideas are classical in automata theory: Myhill–Nerode equivalence identifies the minimum number of deterministic automaton states needed to preserve all future continuations. Predictive/causal-state constructions similarly group histories by future predictive equivalence.

References / neighbours:

- Myhill–Nerode theorem and minimal deterministic automata.
- Computational-mechanics causal-state literature, which explicitly notes analogy with Nerode future equivalence.

### What is stronger in the current CCOC family

For every `m>=1`, the fixed-regular relay simultaneously holds constant:

- primitive action alphabet size;
- one-state closed/open grammar schema, with one transition edit;
- bounded degree;
- one-edge focal/exterior physical cut;
- bounded local alphabets;
- radius-one dynamics;

while achieving

- `|P_C|=2`;
- `|P_O|=2^(m+1)`;
- exact innovation `K_O-K_C=m`;
- equality with the finite-domain upper bound;
- logarithmic canonical access length.

It therefore yields a no-static-resource-only-bound corollary inside the declared finite class. A separate coherence theorem gives a positive sufficient boundary for one portable macro-law.

### Do not claim

- invention of future equivalence or automata minimization;
- historical firstness of bounded-local machine compilation without deeper audit;
- that narrow real ecological corridors secretly store many bits.

### Foreground

**The simultaneous extremal package:** arbitrary exact future-response memory becomes exposed after a one-transition grammar opening while multiple obvious local/static complexity proxies stay uniformly bounded, and the bound is attained sharply.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Deep audit against automata minimization, transducers, sequential-machine composition, communication/interface complexity, causal states and modular dynamical systems. This is one of the chapters where a serious novelty search is worth the effort before final journal claims.

## Chapter 6 / CREST — capability gain versus knowledge burden

### Nearest classical substrate

Cybernetics already connects regulation to required variety and internal models. Ashby's law of requisite variety links regulatory success to available variety/information, and Conant & Ashby's good-regulator theorem formalizes a model requirement for maximally successful simple regulators. State abstraction/bisimulation literatures also study how much state is required to preserve control-relevant behavior.

References:

- Ashby, W. R. (1956), *An Introduction to Cybernetics*, Law of Requisite Variety.
- Conant, R. C. & Ashby, W. R. (1970), *Every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system*, International Journal of Systems Science 1:89–97, DOI `10.1080/00207727008920220`.
- Givan, R., Dean, T. & Greig, M. (2003), *Equivalence notions and model minimization in Markov decision processes*, Artificial Intelligence 147:163–223, DOI `10.1016/S0004-3702(02)00376-4`.

### What CREST proves

A connected finite family exists for every `m` in which:

- one new controllable action is admitted;
- robust carrier gain is exactly one world;
- a retained present slice refines from one required state to `2^m`;
- monitoring debt increases from zero to exactly `m` bits under unchanged evidence;
- full-state reporting becomes unlicensed;
- a declared coarse target remains deterministically reportable.

Hence no finite bound depending only on carrier-size gain can bound required resolution.

### Do not claim

- invention of requisite variety or good-regulator principles;
- that every small management intervention creates large monitoring debt;
- that `m` bits are empirical monitoring cost;
- that full-state knowledge is always needed for useful management reporting.

### Foreground

**A sharp cross-gate separation:** capability increment, state-resolution burden, evidence sufficiency and target reportability can diverge arbitrarily inside one connected construction even when the visible capability gain is fixed at its minimum nonzero size.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Audit cybernetics, sufficient-information control, POMDP/MDP state abstraction, bisimulation, viability/control kernels, management state variables, and monitoring-design literatures. The no-bound separation and retained coarse-target reportability are the points to defend as the distinctive package.

## Chapter 8 / CED — depth versus failure diversity in evidence allocation

### Nearest classical substrate

Ecological imperfect-detection and occupancy design already treats replication as a resource-allocation problem. MacKenzie & Royle (2005) and Bailey et al. (2007) discuss how repeated surveys should be allocated, and explicitly warn that observer/time design can induce correlated detection heterogeneity. Occupancy designs also use multiple observers/methods as alternative replication structures.

Reliability engineering has a separate common-mode-failure/diversity literature: redundancy with correlated/common-mode failures can perform much worse than independence suggests, and diversity is explicitly studied as a mitigation strategy.

References:

- MacKenzie, D. I. et al. (2002), *Estimating site occupancy rates when detection probabilities are less than one*, Ecology 83:2248–2255.
- MacKenzie, D. I. & Royle, J. A. (2005), *Designing occupancy studies: general advice and allocating survey effort*, Journal of Applied Ecology 42.
- Bailey, L. L. et al. (2007), *Sampling design trade-offs in occupancy studies with imperfect detection*, Ecological Applications 17:281–290.
- Littlewood, B. (1996), *The impact of diversity upon common mode failures*, Reliability Engineering & System Safety 51:101–113, DOI `10.1016/0951-8320(95)00120-4`.

### What CED proves

Under one explicit common-mode imperfect-detection contract:

- the general all-coordinate joint-detection frontier is exact;
- for exactly two reads per coordinate, depth versus independent-mode diversity reverses at
  `p*=2-2^(1/k)`;
- both strict sides occur;
- the threshold increases with target dimension;
- at fixed mode count, unlimited within-mode repetition converges to the worst-case availability ceiling `1-(1-a)^m`;
- a target confidence therefore imposes a necessary mode floor;
- target-safe reporting remains separate from raw world-information gain.

### Do not claim

- invention of imperfect-detection replication design;
- discovery of common-mode failure;
- that diversity is always better;
- that the simple threshold formula is historically new without a focused search;
- that declared availability/sensitivity parameters are estimated by the theorem.

### Foreground

**A target-specific exact allocation boundary plus a separate asymptotic guarantee ceiling**, embedded in a reporting framework that distinguishes nominal record refinement from trustworthy target resolution.

### Remaining prior-art burden

Search specifically for closed-form depth-versus-independent-mode allocation thresholds under common-mode detection models, not just general occupancy trade-offs. If the exact `2-2^(1/k)` boundary has a direct predecessor, present it as a derived design corollary and retain CED's broader failure/reporting integration as the contribution.

## Publication rule to add everywhere

Before any phrase such as `novel theorem`, `first`, `new mathematical principle`, or `previously unknown`, require a chapter-specific nearest-neighbour citation audit.

Absent that audit, use one of:

- `we prove, for the declared model class, ...`;
- `we derive an exact condition for ...`;
- `we construct a sharp finite witness in which ...`;
- `we translate an established mathematical substrate into an ecological identification/design consequence ...`;
- `we recover a source-specific empirical boundary under a frozen protocol ...`.

Correctness of a theorem licenses the result. It does not by itself license a priority claim.
