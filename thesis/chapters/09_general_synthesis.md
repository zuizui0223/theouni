# General Synthesis — The Theory Universe: Adequacy Has No Privileged Direction of Travel

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `theouni` synthesis  
**Primary module:** TU-1

## Problem

Each research chapter constructs or audits a representation adequate for a particular ecological responsibility. The synthesis asks whether a state, law, inferential design, evidence result, causal conclusion, loss representation, or warning may be reused after the scientific task or empirical responsibility changes.

## Headline result

For an old stored partition \(P\) and revised required partition \(Q\), exact state-only revision is possible exactly when

\[
q_Q=f\circ q_P.
\]

When this factorization fails, TU-1 gives the exact minimum same-carrier auxiliary alphabet needed for revision and distinguishes worst local hidden splitting from average global refinement.

The empirical synchronization adds a second, non-theorem transport discipline:

```text
RequiredState
    -> MethodValidity
    -> DeploymentDetectability
    -> ObservationSupport
    -> IdentifiedEvidence
    -> ReportableTarget
```

These arrows are bridges, not equalities. A result earned at one stage cannot silently inherit the status of the next.

## Why the result is nontrivial

The synthesis does not claim that all source programmes are one theorem. It identifies the common transport question while preserving the different reasons for failure: capability expansion, open futures, replacement, mechanism disagreement, inferential detectability, realized measurement support, evidence identification, reliability, learning objectives, loss representation, and warning portability.

The new observation-support bridge matters because it separates two failures that are easy to conflate:

```text
procedure cannot recover the target on the declared design
        !=
procedure was qualified, but realized measurement no longer supplies the design it requires
```

The second is an empirical bridge failure, not a biological negative.

## Bounded empirical witness

TTF v0.11 -> v0.12 supplies one source-owned witness of the distinction. A fresh 250-species × 100-record density-scaled design passed its predeclared method/design qualification. Before the empirical statistic was opened, the same frozen photo IDs entered an independent location-blind measurement pipeline. The classifiability/support gate required all 250 species to retain at least 40 evaluable photographs; only 189 did. Colour vectors were not read by TTF, pairwise colour distances were not computed, and no empirical transfer statistic existed.

Therefore the legal endpoint was:

```text
not_evaluable
```

not:

```text
no shared flower-colour transition structure
```

This witness does not empirically validate the Theory Universe. It demonstrates that the bridge discipline can have an observable, fail-closed consequence in a source-owned workflow.

## Ecological payoff

Scientific compression creates future obligations, and empirical implementation creates additional admission obligations. A representation can be fully adequate today yet insufficient for a later management question if revision-relevant distinctions were discarded. Likewise, an estimator can be qualified for a declared target yet fail to yield an empirical answer if realized measurement does not preserve the support on which that qualification depended.

The combined lesson is:

> **Scientific adequacy travels only through explicit bridges, and different bridge failures license different endings.**

A missing state distinction may require auxiliary information. An unresolved evidence distinction may motivate new observation. A reliability failure may require abstention. An observation-support failure may terminate the empirical claim before its statistic exists. A warning may fail because it lacks discrimination even when its precursor is reproducible.

## Canonical synthesis loop

```text
1. declare the responsibility
2. determine required distinctions
3. qualify the inferential procedure on declared worlds
4. qualify deployment detectability
5. verify realized observation support before opening the empirical statistic
6. characterize retained evidence and structural identification
7. acquire additional observation if justified
8. apply reliability-qualified reporting
9. validate measured states/proxies/warnings against future endpoints
10. on contract change, audit whether the earned representation/result is reusable
```

The loop has no privileged direction of travel: later tasks may require refinement, different evidence, a different carrier, a new warning denominator, or a return to raw observations. The important invariant is that every move has a typed bridge and a claim ceiling.

## Claim ceiling

Do not equate scientific forgetting with physical irreversibility, promote pairwise non-conflict to global consistency, claim empirical cost from idealized revision bits, or subsume MLTR's carrier-changing transport into same-carrier TU-1. Do not claim that the `MethodValidity / DeploymentDetectability / ObservationSupport` decomposition is a novel universal theorem or the only valid empirical workflow. Do not treat TTF v0.11/v0.12 as proof of CREST, CED, or the Theory Universe, and do not infer flower-colour presence/absence from its `not_evaluable` endpoint. TU-1 becomes an independent research chapter only after a distinct carrier-changing revision theorem is established.

## Transition

The dissertation closes by replacing the search for one privileged ecological state with a typed rule: scientific representations and inference claims travel only through explicit bridges; a failed bridge determines what must stop or be repaired, and failure to travel is not the same as contradiction or a negative ecological result.
