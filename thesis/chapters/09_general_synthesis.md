# General Synthesis — The Theory Universe: Adequacy Has No Privileged Direction of Travel

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `theouni` synthesis  
**Primary module:** TU-1

## Problem

Each research chapter constructs or audits a representation adequate for a particular ecological responsibility. The synthesis asks whether a state, law, evidence result, causal conclusion, loss representation, or warning may be reused after the scientific task changes.

## Headline result

For an old stored partition \(P\) and revised required partition \(Q\), exact state-only revision is possible exactly when

\[
q_Q=f\circ q_P.
\]

When this factorization fails, TU-1 gives the exact minimum same-carrier auxiliary alphabet needed for revision and distinguishes worst local hidden splitting from average global refinement.

## Why the result is nontrivial

The synthesis does not claim that all source programmes are one theorem. It identifies the common transport question while preserving the different reasons for failure: capability expansion, open futures, replacement, mechanism disagreement, evidence limits, learning objectives, loss representation, and warning portability.

## Ecological payoff

Scientific compression creates future obligations. A representation can be fully adequate today yet irrecoverably insufficient for a later management question if raw worlds or revision-sufficient side information were not retained.

## Claim ceiling

Do not equate scientific forgetting with physical irreversibility, promote pairwise non-conflict to global consistency, claim empirical cost from idealized revision bits, or subsume MLTR's carrier-changing transport into same-carrier TU-1. TU-1 becomes an independent research chapter only after a distinct carrier-changing revision theorem is established.
