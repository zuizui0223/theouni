# General Introduction — The Reuse Problem in Ecology

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `theouni` synthesis only

## Problem

Ecology routinely reuses state labels, reduced laws, monitoring variables, inferential procedures, causal interpretations, and warning indicators beyond the task, model world, sampling design, or domain for which they were first built. The introduction asks when that reuse is scientifically licensed.

Use only the minimum common formal language:

\[
R\models\alpha
\iff
\Sigma_\alpha=f\circ R.
\]

A representation is adequate for task `alpha` when the task-complete response can be reconstructed from what was retained.

For empirical transport, keep a second typed chain explicit:

```text
RequiredState
    != MethodValidity
    != DeploymentDetectability
    != ObservationSupport
    != IdentifiedEvidence
    != ReportableTarget
```

The point is not that every chapter implements every gate. It is that success at one responsibility does not license silent transport into the next.

## Headline result

The dissertation is a theory of transport and non-transport of scientific representations across changed ecological responsibilities, not a catalogue of competing ecological-state definitions.

The synchronized empirical corollary is:

> **A representation or inference rule may be adequate in a declared model/deployment responsibility while the realized observation process fails to preserve the support needed to instantiate that responsibility empirically. Such a workflow terminates as non-evaluable rather than converting missing admissibility into a biological negative.**

TTF v0.11 -> v0.12 may be used only as the bounded source-owned illustration of that corollary: its method/design gate passed, its realized classifiability/support gate failed, and no empirical TTF statistic was opened.

## Why the result is nontrivial

Purpose relativity, abstraction, hidden mechanisms, historical contingency, evidence limits, statistical validity and missing measurement support are individually familiar. The dissertation earns coherence only by showing distinct, source-owned conditions under which reuse fails, succeeds, requires repair, or must stop before a downstream claim exists.

A particularly important non-equivalence is:

```text
qualified to answer if the declared evidence exists
        !=
the declared evidence actually survived measurement
```

This prevents a method PASS from being narrated as empirical evidence and prevents an observation-support FAIL from being narrated as an ecological null.

## Ecological payoff

The framing turns a broad state debate into auditable questions: What changed? Which response must still factor through the retained representation? Was the inferential design qualified for that response? Did realized measurement preserve enough support to instantiate it? What is identified now? Which bridge is required before a previous result can travel or a report can be issued?
