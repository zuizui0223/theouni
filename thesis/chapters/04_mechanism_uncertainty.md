# Chapter 4 — When Visible Equivalence Fails under Mechanism Uncertainty

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `zuizui0223/mrm`

## Problem

Several retained mechanisms may generate the same visible ecological state while implying different responses to a contemplated intervention. The chapter asks what deterministic law, typed law, or set-valued report remains scientifically honest.

## Headline result

A candidate-independent deterministic law exists exactly when retained mechanism response types agree on every declared future response. When they disagree, the coarsest candidate-safe quotient retains only response-relevant mechanism distinctions and reporting remains typed or set-valued.

## Why the result is nontrivial

The chapter does not equate ecological state with full mechanism identity. Mechanism differences matter only where they change the response demanded by the task, while visible equivalence alone cannot justify erasing such disagreement.

## Ecological payoff

Model uncertainty can be represented without either pretending that one mechanism is known or carrying every microscopic mechanism detail. The relevant unit is the intervention-response type.

## Claim ceiling

Do not claim that MRM infers the true mechanism family, candidate priors, empirical validity, or CED-style reliability and false-resolution guarantees. Generic robust-control and partition-refinement substrate is not the novelty claim.
