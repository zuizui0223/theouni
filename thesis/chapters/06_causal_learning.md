# Chapter 6 — When Learning the Cause and Licensing the Decision Diverge

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `zuizui0223/microdonta` / RACH  
**Embedded firewall:** TU-2

## Problem

Observed ecological patterns often remain compatible with several causal programmes. The chapter asks how to retain that multiplicity, choose the next observation, and avoid confusing causal learning with immediate target licensing.

## Headline result

RACH retains all causal programmes compatible with the declared model family, biological constraints, and observations, then ranks feasible measurements by validated next-observation value. In the frozen budget-2 benchmark, RACH-SEQ resolved all initial confounding edges on average and converged in 99.0% of systems, versus 60.45% edge resolution and 43.5% convergence under random order, with no hidden-truth exclusion. TU-2 shows that causal-learning value and target-licensing status can vary independently and reverse equal-cost experiment rankings.

## Why the result is nontrivial

The practical method and the firewall work together: a measurement can be excellent for identifying mechanism and useless for the current report target, or vice versa. “More informative” has no untyped meaning.

## Ecological payoff

The chapter supplies a defensible next-observation workflow while preserving the difference between understanding a mechanism and making a conservation decision.

## Claim ceiling

Do not promote RACH to a best-model selector, treat pattern fit as causal proof, assume a proxy measures a causal channel without stable calibration, or infer target licensing from NOV alone.
