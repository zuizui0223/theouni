## Chapter thesis

A fixed small increase in conservation capability can force an arbitrarily large increase in the distinctions that an adequate present-state representation and monitoring system must resolve.

## Forbidden inference

\[
\boxed{
\text{small intervention or capability gain}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{small epistemic burden}
}
\]

The chapter must target an actual error pattern: treating project size, restored area, intervention count, carrier gain, or a fixed project-cost percentage as if it certified adequate monitoring resolution. Administrative budgeting rules may remain useful; they are not epistemic bounds.
