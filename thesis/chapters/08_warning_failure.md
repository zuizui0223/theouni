# Chapter 8 — When an Early Signal Is Not a Warning

**Status:** source-bounded writing brief  
**Primary owner:** `zuizui0223/eco-genetic-warning-extensions`  
**Embedded firewall:** TU-4

## Problem

A signal can repeatedly precede an adverse event yet fail as a warning if it also fires when the event does not occur. The chapter evaluates warning only after the loss-generating domain has been frozen without inspecting warning outcomes.

## Headline result

Baseline-relative genetic erosion preceded realised functional loss in all inherited 35/35 and fresh 33/33 event trajectories across the six frozen endpoints. The same thresholds also fired in all inherited 48/48 and fresh 49/49 non-event trajectories, producing full-horizon specificity zero and binary-marker AUC 0.5. TU-4 separates LossGeneratingState from WarningEvaluationState and within-state validity from cross-state portability.

## Why the result is nontrivial

The temporal ordering genuinely replicated, yet prediction failed when the full denominator was restored. This is a stronger scientific conclusion than either a preferred positive warning result or a generic caution about false positives.

## Ecological payoff

A defensible warning must discriminate events from non-events within a warning-adequate state. It must then pass a separate portability test before being used across eco-genetic states, habitats, or numerical thresholds.

## Claim ceiling

Do not claim a universal genetic threshold, predictive warning validity, direction-only warning causation, complete natural state, or cross-state portability. Natural partial-state tests remain bounded and endpoint-specific.
