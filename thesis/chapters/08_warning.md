## Chapter thesis

A genetic signal can precede functional loss in every event trajectory and reproduce under fresh seeds while failing completely as a warning when it also fires in every non-event. Warning ordering, discrimination, state sufficiency, and cross-state portability are distinct claims.

## Forbidden inference

\[
\boxed{
	ext{signal precedes loss}
\not\Rightarrow
	ext{the signal is a valid or portable warning}
}
\]

## Source-owned empirical and simulation results

- all 35 inherited event trajectories and all 33 fresh event trajectories showed baseline-relative \(H_\alpha/H_\gamma\) erosion before each fixed loss endpoint;
- the same six thresholds fired in all 48 inherited and all 49 fresh non-event trajectories;
- full-horizon specificity was zero and the binary marker AUC was 0.5;
- the result is replicated event-conditional ordering, not predictive warning validity;
- connectivity-heterogeneity and partner-related signals were bounded, non-replicated, or non-portable under the declared alternative closures;
- candidate natural state variables must earn status through held-out endpoint information before origin or history is tested for residual gain.

## Embedded TU-4 role

TU-4 separates two response signatures:

\[
Q_L\preceq Q_W,
\]

where the warning-evaluation state refines the loss-generating state. Equality requires the warning response to factor through the loss quotient. Even when warning is well-defined inside matched loss states, cross-domain portability requires an additional correspondence that preserves the warning law.

TU-4 is a type and transport firewall, not a standalone generic factorization novelty claim.

## Prohibited upgrades

- The fixed relative thresholds are validated predictors.
- A signal that reliably leads loss events is necessarily an early warning.
- Within-state replication proves a universal threshold or cross-state portability.
- Historical screen labels are biological warning regimes.
- Access failure, metadata failure, or non-identifiability is an ecological null.
- Candidate empirical proxies are granted state status by plausibility alone.
