## Chapter thesis

Causal ambiguity should be retained as a set of still-admissible programmes and reduced by observations chosen for causal learning. But the observation that best resolves the causal programme need not be the observation that licenses the requested ecological target.

## Forbidden inference

\[
\boxed{
	ext{pattern fit or high causal-learning value}
\not\Rightarrow
	ext{identified cause or licensed decision target}
}
\]

## Source-owned RACH results

- N1: net-only observations of \(W=FE\) are structurally non-identifying.
- N2: net performance plus one exact channel identifies the other in the positive model.
- N3/N4: a proxy identifies relative channel change only under stable or separately calibrated conversion.
- RACH retains all causal programmes compatible with the model family, constraints, observation map, and tolerance.
- RACH-SEQ selects observations by validated next-observation value.
- In the frozen budget-2 benchmark, RACH-SEQ resolved all initial confounding edges on average and converged in 99.0% of systems, versus 60.45% edge resolution and 43.5% convergence under random order, with zero hidden-truth exclusion.

## Embedded TU-2 result

TU-2 supplies a bridge firewall rather than a standalone chapter:

\[
\boxed{
	ext{CausalLearningValue}
\neq
	ext{TargetLicensingStatus}
}
\]

Equal-cost experiments can have equal causal information and opposite licensing, maximal causal learning with no target license, or zero causal learning with complete target license.
