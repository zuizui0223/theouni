## Chapter thesis

An exact ecological macro-law can fail after turnover, extinction, recolonization, habitat reconfiguration, or rewiring; when it fails, the relevant problem is not unconstrained target minimization but the unique coarsest exact repair that preserves every inherited source merge still valid in the target.

## Forbidden inference

\[
\boxed{
	ext{exact source macro-law}
\not\Rightarrow
	ext{the same exact macro-law after structural replacement}
}
\]
