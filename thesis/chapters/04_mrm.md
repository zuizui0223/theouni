# Chapter 4 — When Visible Equivalence Fails under Mechanism Uncertainty

**Status:** G2 source/claim map fixed.  
**Primary owner:** `zuizui0223/mrm`.  
**Worldline:** Mechanism / Response Type.

## Chapter thesis

When several mechanisms remain compatible with the same visible ecological state, a candidate-independent deterministic law exists exactly when their response types agree on every future response required by the task. When they disagree, typed or set-valued reporting is stronger science than an unsupported single law.

## Forbidden inference

\[
\boxed{
	ext{same visible present state}
\not\Rightarrow
	ext{same deterministic intervention law}
}
\]

## Source-owned results

- necessary-and-sufficient agreement criterion for a candidate-independent deterministic law;
- typed deterministic and candidate-forgetting set-valued reports;
- unique coarsest observation-preserving candidate-safe quotient;
- mechanism-ambiguity frontier with state-memory and intervention-depth witnesses;
- active and positive-cost discrimination when the action grammar permits it.

## Central conceptual move

MRM does not insist that every mechanistic difference belongs in ecological state. Two mechanisms may remain merged when their required response types agree. State refinement is caused by response disagreement, not by hiddenness or microscopic difference alone.
