# Consolidated publication strategy — 2026-09-06

## Objective

Reduce publication fragmentation. The source repositories remain independent proof/evidence owners, but the default submission plan is a small number of question-centered papers.

This strategy is subordinate to scientific coherence: a source manuscript is retained as a fallback artifact even when it is not the default submission unit.

## Paper 1 — CREST flagship theory paper

### Working title

**When Conservation Capacity Outgrows Conservation Knowledge: A Contract-Relative Theory of Ecological State**

### First-choice journal

**The American Naturalist — Major Article**

Current journal guidance emphasizes broad conceptual unification across ecology/evolution/behavior and welcomes Major Articles containing new theory. The current instructions normally expect Major Articles to remain around 7,500 words or less, excluding Literature Cited, and at no more than six figures/tables unless extra length is justified.

Current sources checked 2026-09-06:

- https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/an/instruct
- https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/journals/an/editorial

### Why this is the target

The integrated CREST paper is not strongest as a collection of partition/refinement theorems. Its strongest contribution is one conceptual problem:

> **A new conservation capability can improve what can be done while making the old state description and monitoring system scientifically insufficient.**

The flagship theorem fixes capability gain at one viable world while allowing the required resolution/monitoring debt to grow by arbitrary `m` bits. CCOC, MLTR, MRM, and CED then show distinct reasons or scientific gates through which an old state merge can fail.

This architecture matches a conceptual-unification journal better than four or five narrower source-repository papers.

### Main-text budget

To fit a Major Article, the main paper should foreground:

1. the conservation/state problem and contract-relative state;
2. one flagship finite theorem and its ecological meaning;
3. one compact result for each obstruction class — future/composition, structural history, retained mechanism;
4. evidence licensing as the final gate;
5. one ecological worked example / interpretation;
6. claim ceiling and scope.

Move full theorem registries, long proofs, alternative constructions, active-discrimination algorithms, and CED allocation/calibration details to Online Supplementary Material.

### Fallback journal

**Theoretical Ecology** if review indicates that the paper is too theorem-dense or too narrowly formal for the broader conceptual-unification threshold of The American Naturalist.

The fallback should not trigger scientific fragmentation. The integrated CREST manuscript remains one paper.

## Paper 2 — Identification + observation-design methods paper

### Working title

**From Identifiability Limits to Mechanism-Resolving Observation Design**

### First-choice journal

**Methods in Ecology and Evolution — Research Article**

Current MEE scope explicitly treats analytical, practical, and conceptual developments as methods, emphasizes the method rather than an ecological application result, and asks computational methods to include simulation or benchmark testing. Research Articles are expected to be broadly applicable across taxa/systems rather than restricted to one case.

Current sources checked 2026-09-06:

- https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/2041210x/aims-and-scope/read-full-aims-and-scope
- https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/hub/journal/2041210x/author-guidelines

### Why integration helps MEE fit

Boundary alone is an identification Perspective; MROD alone is an observation-selection method. Their integrated design question is stronger:

```text
Can a proposed measurement change structural identifiability at all?
        -> exact rank / observation-map gate
If ambiguity remains and several verified candidates can distinguish it,
which measurement should be acquired next?
        -> information value + sequential recomputation
```

The primary validation remains MROD's frozen truth-peek-free controlled benchmark. The Boundary pollination-style witness supplies an interpretable structural-identification example, not a separate empirical claim.

### Main-text budget

1. two-gate mechanism-resolving measurement problem;
2. observation-rank theorem and one ecological witness;
3. admissible mechanism region and observation information value;
4. adaptive recomputation condition;
5. frozen controlled benchmark;
6. practical reporting/stopping rules and limitations.

Do not turn the manuscript into a concatenation of the existing Boundary and MROD drafts. Calibration-transport detail and extended proofs belong in SI.

## Conditional Paper 3 — integrated eco-genetic state validity

### Working title

**Eco-genetic state validity is responsibility-specific**

### Status

**Conditional, not default.**

The chapter is scientifically coherent as a paper because state separation, transition sufficiency/propagation, and warning discrimination all ask whether the same eco-genetic representation supports different responsibilities. However, the default portfolio target is two primary papers.

Promote this to an independent submission only if one of the following is true after the CREST main-text audit:

1. the eco-genetic material is too extensive to serve as a compact worked scientific layer around the broader state theory;
2. its finite-model and warning-denominator results form a stronger domain-specific narrative than can be represented honestly inside CREST;
3. removing it from CREST materially improves the flagship paper's conceptual focus without leaving the eco-genetic paper dependent on CREST for its core logic.

### Hard publication firewall

Even if integrated into one eco-genetic paper:

- common-scalar failure does not imply warning failure;
- transition propagation does not validate the warning statistic;
- warning failure does not prove the five-state taxonomy;
- the natural-data four-gate programme does not become empirical validation of the finite model.

Existing EGC, warning-only, and state-validity manuscripts/bundles remain fallback assets.

## What is no longer the default submission plan

Do not automatically submit:

- CCOC as one paper,
- CREST as a second,
- MLTR as a third,
- MRM as a fourth,
- CED as a fifth,
- Boundary and MROD separately,
- EGC, state-validity, and warning-validity as three separate papers.

Their source manuscripts remain useful for proof development, peer-review provenance, contingency submissions, and extracting SI modules. They are not publication obligations.

## Decision rule before any standalone source-paper submission

A source-paper submission requires a written answer to all four questions:

1. **Distinct question:** does this paper answer a question that is not already the natural section-level question of an integrated manuscript?
2. **Independent evidence:** does it have evidence/proof sufficient for the standalone claim without borrowing novelty or motivation from the integrated paper?
3. **Non-overlap:** can its introduction, theorem statements, and discussion be written without substantial text/result duplication with the flagship paper?
4. **Portfolio gain:** does separate submission increase scientific clarity enough to justify an additional paper rather than merely increasing paper count?

If any answer is no, default to integration.
