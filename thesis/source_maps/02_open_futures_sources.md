## B. Prior-art boundary

### Source-checked Related Work

**Source:** `zuizui0223/ccoc:manuscript/related_work.md`

Supports the following ancestry:

- continuation-based state equivalence and fixed-task minimization are classical substrate;
- incompletely specified-machine minimization already makes state reduction specification-dependent;
- interacting-FSM methods use environment-imposed input restrictions and permissible behaviours;
- state reduction and machine realization/composition can fail to commute.

Verified records currently include Paull and Unger (1959), Hartmanis and Stearns (1962), Wang and Brayton (1993), Aziz et al. (1993), and Watanabe and Brayton (1993).

### Defensible novelty statement

The dissertation chapter may claim the explicit quantitative package:

\[
|P_C|=2,
\qquad
|P_O|=2^{m+1},
\qquad
K_O-K_C=m,
\]

for one newly legal primitive action in one fixed controlled family, together with bounded-local constraints and logarithmic access.

It must not claim that context-sensitive minimization, noncommutation, generic pair separation, or modular realization is new in isolation.

## C. Boundary from CREST Chapter 1

### CREST object

CREST compares:

- gain in robustly controllable worlds;
- least-state complexity on a retained present slice;
- evidence/monitoring debt;
- target reportability.

Its headline fixes \(\Delta|K^*|=1\) while \(\Delta K_{U_0}=m\).

### CCOC object

CCOC compares:

\[
K_{\mathrm{open}}
\quad\text{with}\quad
\max_j K_{\mathrm{closed},j},
\]

where each closed grammar can have its own independently optimized exact interface and the physical controlled plant is held fixed.

### Allowed connection

Chapter 2 may state that future addressability is a structural mechanism by which dormant distinctions become required information.

### Prohibited collapse

Do not:

- treat CCOC as the proof of CREST's carrier/evidence theorem;
- import CREST evidence licensing or reportability into CCOC's headline;
- rewrite the CCOC gap as carrier gain;
- describe the two results as one theorem with two names.

## D. Boundary from MLTR Chapter 3

### CCOC

- underlying controlled plant fixed;
- legal future grammar changed;
- closed interfaces independently optimized;
- open/closed minimum-interface gap is headline.

### MLTR

- source and target systems may be non-nested;
- one inherited source partition is fixed;
- target repair must preserve every inherited merge still valid;
- source-relative transport, repair, defect, and history completion are headline.

### Transition

Chapter 2 ends when the system itself is replaced. At that point a grammar-only comparison no longer answers whether an inherited macro-law retains its meaning. That motivates MLTR without turning target replacement into another CCOC instance.

## E. Ecological interpretation boundary

The ecological readings—colonization, corridor reopening, source accessibility, interaction-partner arrival, delayed reservoirs, or rewiring—are theoretical projections of the grammar contract.

They do not establish:

- that a field system has \(m\) independently addressable exterior bits;
- that a real network realizes the relay tree;
- that exact open-interface inflation is common;
- that narrow physical cuts imply large ecological memory;
- that observed variables identify the correct grammar.

Empirical application requires an independent Reality-to-Theory bridge defining states, focal output, legal futures, and response interpretation.
