# Submission architecture — observation / evidence / mechanism series

Status: canonical portfolio submission plan, 2026-09-08.

## Core rule

Do not submit the repository graph as one omnibus paper. Preserve ownership by scientific question.

The program is split into two paper series that meet at a later synthesis.

## Series O — Observation information operations

### O1 — V3: retained-information refinement
Owner: `zuizui0223/v3`
Question: when does retained side/reference information legitimately refine compatible states without destructive suppression?
Primary ownership: refinement, reversible decomposition, physical-proxy realizability, reference portfolios.
Do not own: target-safe reportability, mechanism-design utility, upstream empirical entry bias, semantic-interface performance.

### O2 — REC: record-entry selection
Owner: `zuizui0223/rec`
Question: what changes when opportunities fail to become rows, and when can entry provenance repair the ecological estimand?
Primary ownership: external exposure denominator, shadow/no-row support, irreversibility of omitted rows, correction/transport domain.
Do not own: downstream semantic-vocabulary benefit or general compatible-world theory as novelty.

### O3 — TNOA: semantic preservation / coarsening
Owner: `zuizui0223/tnoa`
Question: what evidence state should be retained before ecological inference, and what information is destroyed by premature semantic collapse?
Primary ownership: positive non-complementary target/nuisance evidence, observability/attribution separation, unresolved state, closed-world coarsening result.
Do not own: pre-entry selection or general future-observation design.

## Series E — Evidence licensing and mechanism resolution

### E1 — Boundary: current identification limit
Owner: `zuizui0223/boundary`
Question: what mechanism distinctions can the current observation map identify in principle?
Primary ownership: observational equivalence, rank/identified-set boundary, new identification direction versus precision.
Do not own: optimal next measurement or target-relative reporting risk.

### E2 — MROD: mechanism-learning observation design
Owner: `zuizui0223/mrod`
Question: among currently compatible mechanisms, which candidate observation maximizes expected mechanism information?
Primary ownership: residual mechanism ambiguity, normalized mechanism information value, sequential information-guided acquisition.
Utility: causal/mechanism learning.
Do not own: false-resolution target licensing or current structural boundary as a separate novelty claim.

### E3 — CED Paper B: target licensing / reportability
Owner: `zuizui0223/ced`
Question: given finite evidence and a declared target, which distinctions must be resolved to license a report, and which experiment should be chosen under failure/risk/cost constraints?
Primary ownership: experiment-induced quotient, target-safe quotient, failure-aware trustworthy refinement, risk-limited adaptive reporting.
Utility: target licensing.
Do not own: generic partition refinement as novelty or mechanism entropy maximization.

## Critical firewall: MROD versus CED

MROD optimizes mechanism-learning value.
CED governs target-licensing status under explicit false-resolution and cost contracts.

More mechanism information need not produce more target licensing, and a target-safe experiment need not maximize full mechanism entropy reduction.

This distinction is already represented in theouni as `CausalLearningValue != TargetLicensingStatus`.

## Layer-1 umbrella paper

The current V3 Layer-1 draft must not be submitted as an omnibus Research Article before the specialist papers because it would pre-empt their novelty.

Near-term role:
- internal canonical synthesis;
- optional public preprint only after overlap firewall language is added;
- U1–U3 (refinement / selection / coarsening) may remain as its primary synthetic witnesses;
- prospective-design U4 and intervention U5 should be extensions/corroboration rather than headline novelty because CED/MROD/InsePi own those scientific questions.

Later role:
- a Perspective/Synthesis after the specialist papers are submitted or published;
- central statement: scientific observation systems manage compatible-world distinctions through information addition, support deletion, semantic coarsening, current identification limits and future acquisition.

## Physical systems

`pollipi` and `insepi` are validation platforms, not definitions of the Layer-1 theory.

- InsePi V13: Layer-2 blinded physical validation of observation-system intervention/refinement claims.
- PolliPi field work: Layer-3 natural observation validation including real targets, wind, shadow, flower motion and record-entry effects.

Do not hold the synthetic/theory papers until these layers finish unless the target journal explicitly requires physical/field validation.

## Submission order

### Wave 1 — submit mature specialist manuscripts
1. CED Paper B — flagship finite evidence/reportability method.
2. TNOA — process-preserving ecological observation interface.
3. MROD — mechanism-learning sequential observation design.

They must cross-cite each other only through explicit non-overlap statements; do not present the same generic quotient/refinement theorem as separate novelty.

### Wave 2 — empirical and conceptual companions
4. REC H1–H5 — external-data record-entry selection paper.
5. Boundary Paper A — mechanistic-identification Perspective.

### Wave 3 — synthesis and physical closure
6. Layer-1 information-order Perspective/Synthesis, narrowed to cross-stage information operations and the common compatible-world geometry.
7. InsePi V13 physical paper if/when the frozen held-out result materializes.
8. PolliPi natural-field observation paper after prospective truth/reference data exist.

## Journal routing principle

Avoid placing CED, TNOA and MROD simultaneously at the same journal if their abstracts still share substantial wording. Stagger or route them to different journals.

- MEE is well matched to broad analytical/conceptual methods with simulation/benchmark validation.
- Ecological Informatics is a plausible route for observation/information-processing methods and software-heavy work.
- A broad mechanistic-identification Perspective can be pitched to a general ecology theory journal after a journal-specific scope check.

Final journal choice is downstream of claim ownership; do not change paper boundaries to chase a journal.

## One-sentence portfolio map

Observation series asks what information is added, lost or semantically collapsed; Boundary/MROD ask what mechanism distinctions remain and what to measure for learning; CED asks what evidence is sufficient to license the declared target report; physical systems test whether these distinctions survive real sensing.
