# Future frogcs stage-legacy synthesis — gated research roadmap

2026-10-25. **Decision:** RC6 frogcs/JAE remains an independent submission-ready, scientifically locked empirical paper. Integrate its scientific insight into a *future* ontogenetic and hydroperiod community study, **not by revising RC6 or pooling unverifiable observations**.

## Research programme, not monorepo

The common scientific link is **historically inherited latent ecological state → observation at one stage → future response under environmental change**. The current map contains independent systems, different measured quantities and different strengths of evidence; do not combine their numerical effects as one meta-analysis.

## One prospective target and its experimental hinge

> **Does wetland history leave a living, stage-specific legacy that predicts acoustic site selection and offspring fate beyond local hydrology and permanent site preferences?**

**The intervention/measurement that matters:** add *co-located overwintered tadpole stage, eggs and survival* to a repeated *same-pond* acoustic and water-level series. This bridge is currently missing in NAAMP and NPS. Stage-resolved predator exposure/exclusion and observed dry-reflood transitions separate competing explanations. Previous caller audio is a **potential** proxy to validate, not established predator abundance.

The synthesis's new contribution must be an actual held-out biological prediction or causal discrimination beyond the RC6 old endpoints, not a retrospective narrative containing many repositories. If stage model fails to improve held-out results, the programme must report failure rather than absorb RC6 as mechanistic evidence.

This roadmap is **an active design agreement**, not preregistration. The hypotheses were developed after existing NAAMP observations and side analyses. Truly confirmatory tests require new untouched data and a frozen outcome plan before effect inspection.

## Never-confuse list

- Same-year adult calling \(\neq\) the abundance of tadpoles that consumed eggs later the same spring.
- Matched prior call \(\neq\) verified overwintering larvae.
- Acoustic stop \(\neq\) exactly one pond.
- Relative seasonal survey run number \(\neq\) stage clock.
- ERA5 soil moisture \(\neq\) observed drying of a specific pond.
- Conditional concentration residual \(+0.2969\) \(\neq\) a predator effect estimate.
- GloBI edge/cooccurrence \(\neq\) measured same-pond feeding.
- Toy ABM output \(\neq\) observed biological prevalence.
- Cross-repository theoretical analogy \(\neq\) independently replicated ecological law.
- **One future synthesis manuscript \(\neq\) 52 repositories physically merged.**

## Publication/freeze policy

1. **RC6**: Existing \`frogcs/main\`, \`release/jae-multisite-rc6\`, \`submission/jae-multisite-v6\` retained; no mechanistic stage hypothesis inserted into the RC6 conclusions; archive and author metadata remain in current submission workflow.
2. **Future ecological synthesis**: build in \`frogcs\` non-main research branch; publish when stage × water × calling evidence exists and a novel endpoint is independently demonstrable. The global GloBI stage-network and additional ecological examples may serve as external context, not substitute for direct wetland observations.
3. **Research-universe programme**: \`theouni\` indexes typed adapters/provenance and could later synthesize a *cross-system question about retained ecological state*. Such a separate synthesis follows source paper publication, never supersedes or transfers empirical authorship.
4. **CI/governance**: new research files are append-only by default; never force-push, delete archives or retroactively re-label post-readback analyses as pre-registered.
