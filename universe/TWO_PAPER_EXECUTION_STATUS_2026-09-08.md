## Why the two-paper route was retired

The integrated drafts were mathematically coherent, but consolidation diluted source-level scientific identities:

- TNOA became too close to a generic semantic-coarsening result rather than its core semantic-restraint / unresolved-evidence argument;
- MROD's sequential mechanism-learning design became one branch inside a broader Evidence paper;
- CED's target-safe reportability and failure architecture became entangled with Boundary and MROD ownership;
- V3/REC can be combined more naturally as an ecological observation/information-processing paper without absorbing TNOA.

The cross-repository synthesis is now handled at a higher conceptual level by C2, `More measurement is not more evidence`, rather than by merging all methods into two manuscripts.
