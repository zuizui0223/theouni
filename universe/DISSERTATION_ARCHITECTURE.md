# Preferred dissertation traversal — when ecological representations stop travelling

> **Status:** editorial architecture only. This document selects the chapter order that best exposes novelty. It does not impose a privileged order on the Theory Universe, alter the source theorem DAG, transfer theorem ownership to `theouni`, or close any open empirical bridge.

Machine-readable source: [`dissertation_architecture.json`](dissertation_architecture.json).

## 1. The editorial decision

The dissertation should not be organized as eight variations on the question “what is ecological state?” That framing makes familiar components—purpose-relative adequacy, history dependence, hidden mechanisms, evidence limits, and quotient construction—look like a cumulative framework assembled from known parts.

The stronger organizing problem is:

> **When does a scientific representation that is adequate for one ecological task cease to be reusable after the scientific world changes?**

The dissertation therefore treats the source programmes as distinct **transport-failure problems**:

```text
management capability changes
future grammar opens
system is replaced
mechanism responsibility changes
evidence or target changes
learning objective changes
representation is specialized to loss
warning responsibility is added
old compression is reused after task revision
```

Each research chapter owns one non-obvious forbidden inference. `theouni` appears only in the General Synthesis, where it provides the typed map of bridge conditions and non-transport boundaries.

This is a **preferred editorial traversal**, not the identity of the theory. The underlying Worldline Atlas remains non-linear and admits many valid topological orders.

## 2. General Introduction — The Reuse Problem in Ecology

The Introduction should ask:

> When may a state, law, measurement, causal result, or warning built for one ecological responsibility be reused for another?

Use only the minimum shared formal language:

\[
R\models\alpha
\iff
\Sigma_\alpha=f\circ R.
\]

A representation is adequate for a task when the task-complete response can be reconstructed from what was retained.

Introduce the seven universe-wide invariants, but do not present the full repository catalogue or all nine worldlines. In particular, do not sell purpose relativity, quotient construction, partition refinement, or factorization as the central novelty. They are the common grammar needed to compare the source-owned results.

# Part I — When Doing More Requires Knowing More

## Chapter 1 — When Conservation Capacity Outgrows Conservation Knowledge

**Primary owner:** CREST  
**Worldline:** Capability / Required-State

Headline:

\[
\boxed{
\forall m\ge1:\quad
\Delta |K^*|=1,
\qquad
\Delta K_{U_0}=m.
}
\]

One new controllable action can add exactly one viable world while forcing one retained present state to split into \(2^m\) required states. Under unchanged evidence, the resolution deficit is exactly \(m\) bits.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
small intervention or capability gain
    =>
small required-state or monitoring burden
```

**Why first?**

This is the strongest conservation-facing surprise. It begins from an actual scientific error pattern—treating intervention scale as if it bounded epistemic burden—rather than from a general state framework.

**Chapter endpoint**

> Intervention scale may guide administrative budgeting, but cannot by itself certify that monitoring resolution is adequate.

---

## Chapter 2 — When Closed Simplicity Fails under Open Futures

**Primary owner:** CCOC  
**Worldline:** Future / Open Grammar

Headline:

\[
|P_C|=2,
\qquad
|P_O|=2^{m+1},
\qquad
K_O-K_C=m.
\]

Small exact interfaces under separately fixed closed grammars need not imply a small exact interface after the legal future grammar is opened.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
simple exact interfaces under every closed grammar
    =>
simple exact interface under the open grammar
```

**Boundary from CREST**

- CREST compares capability/carrier gain with required-state and monitoring burden.
- CCOC compares independently optimized closed-grammar interfaces with the jointly open interface.

The chapter explains the structural mechanism beneath capability-resolution divergence without turning CCOC into a CREST corollary.

**Part I conclusion**

> An expansion of what can happen need not be commensurate with an expansion of what must be represented.

# Part II — When Scientific Laws Fail to Travel

## Chapter 3 — When Macro-Laws Do Not Survive Ecological Replacement

**Primary owner:** MLTR  
**Worldline:** History / Replacement

The source and target systems may be non-nested. The chapter asks whether an accepted source macro-law can survive turnover, extinction, recolonization, habitat reconfiguration, or interaction rewiring.

Main results:

- exact replacement transport criterion;
- finite fiber-split obstruction;
- unique coarsest source-relative target repair;
- transport defect;
- path-label coherence;
- minimal history augmentation.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
exact source macro-law
    =>
the same exact macro-law after structural replacement
```

**Distinct novelty**

The target quotient is not optimized from scratch. It is constrained to preserve every inherited source merge that remains valid. This source-relative semantic constraint distinguishes MLTR from generic target aggregation and from CCOC's independently optimized closed/open comparison.

---

## Chapter 4 — When Visible Equivalence Fails under Mechanism Uncertainty

**Primary owner:** MRM  
**Worldline:** Mechanism / Response Type

Headline:

> A candidate-independent deterministic law exists exactly when all retained mechanism response types agree on the declared future responses.

When they do not agree, the honest outputs are typed or set-valued, and the coarsest candidate-safe quotient retains only response-relevant mechanism distinctions.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
same visible present state
    =>
same deterministic intervention law
```

MRM does not require full mechanism identity to define state. Mechanism differences matter only when they alter a response required by the task.

**Part II conclusion**

> Scientific law transport can fail because inherited meaning is broken by replacement or because unresolved mechanisms imply different required responses.

# Part III — When More Information Does Not Give the Needed Answer

## Chapter 5 — When Evidence Does Not License the State We Need

**Primary owner:** CED  
**Worldline:** Evidence / Licensing

The chapter moves from structural adequacy to evidential entitlement:

```text
experiment records
    -> compatible-world quotient
    -> target-safe resolution requirement
    -> failure-aware trustworthy refinement
    -> risk-limited report or honest ambiguity
```

**Forbidden inference**

```text
more, finer, or nominally distinguishing information
    =>
a licensed ecological target
```

The main scientific content is not the equivalence relation itself. It is the coupling of target-relative reporting to failure architecture and false-resolution control. Repetition within one shared failure domain is not equivalent to independent observation modes.

---

## Chapter 6 — When Learning the Cause and Licensing the Decision Diverge

**Primary owner:** RACH  
**Embedded firewall:** TU-2  
**Worldline:** Causal Learning / Next Observation

RACH retains the complete admissible causal set rather than selecting an unsupported winner, then evaluates measurements by next-observation value.

The controlled benchmark supplies an executable result: under the frozen budget-2 comparison, RACH-SEQ resolved all initial confounding edges on average and converged in 99.0% of systems, versus 60.45% edge resolution and 43.5% convergence under random order, without hidden-truth exclusion.

TU-2 belongs here, not in an independent chapter:

\[
\boxed{
	ext{CausalLearningValue}
\neq
	ext{TargetLicensingStatus}.
}
\]

The two objectives can rank equal-cost experiments in opposite order.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
pattern fit or high causal-learning value
    =>
identified cause or licensed decision target
```

**Part III conclusion**

> Required resolution, causal learning, target licensing, and reliability-qualified evidence are different scientific achievements.

# Part IV — When an Early Signal Is Not a Warning

## Chapter 7 — Which State Actually Generates Functional Loss?

**Primary owner:** eco-genetic-criticality  
**Embedded firewall:** TU-3  
**Worldline:** Loss / Dynamic State

The complete explicit simulator state is future-sufficient under the declared simulator closure, but common ecological and genetic marginals are not generally sufficient. Two states can match census, interaction and allele-frequency marginals, realized trait-bin state, \(H_\alpha\), \(H_\gamma\), and \(F_{ST}\), yet differ in the exact next interaction transition because patchwise cross-layer alignment differs.

TU-3 is used only to enforce the representation boundary:

\[
oxed{
	ext{CompleteSimulatorState}
\neq
	ext{LossGeneratingState}.
}
\]

**Forbidden inference**

```text
raw simulator detail or matching coarse marginals
    =>
the correct loss-generating state
```

The chapter must end by fixing the loss domain warning-blind. That is the one hard scientific dependency required before Chapter 8.

---

## Chapter 8 — When an Early Signal Is Not a Warning

**Primary owner:** eco-genetic-warning-extensions  
**Embedded firewall:** TU-4  
**Worldline:** Warning / Portability

The reproduced ordering is deliberately allowed to look successful before the full denominator changes the conclusion:

- genetic erosion preceded realized loss in 35/35 inherited event trajectories;
- the ordering reproduced in 33/33 fresh event trajectories;
- the same thresholds fired in 48/48 inherited and 49/49 fresh non-event trajectories;
- full-horizon specificity was zero;
- the binary marker AUC was 0.5.

Therefore:

\[
oxed{
	ext{signal precedes loss}
\not\Rightarrow
	ext{signal predicts loss}.
}
\]

TU-4 belongs inside this chapter:

\[
Q_L\preceq Q_W,
\]

with equality only when warning response factors through the loss quotient. Within-state replication also does not imply cross-state portability.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
signal leads loss
    =>
valid or portable warning
```

**Part IV conclusion**

> Warning must discriminate events from non-events within a warning-adequate state and pass a separate transport test before becoming a cross-system indicator.

# General Synthesis — The Theory Universe: Adequacy Has No Privileged Direction of Travel

**Primary owner:** theouni / TU-1  
**Worldline:** Revision / After Compression

TU-1 appears here because it changes the interpretation of every previous chapter. Even after a chapter has constructed an adequate representation, later reuse may be impossible if the old representation has already erased distinctions required by the revised task.

For old stored partition \(P\) and revised required partition \(Q\), state-only revision exists exactly when

\[
q_Q=f\circ q_P.
\]

Otherwise TU-1 gives the exact minimum same-carrier auxiliary revision alphabet and distinguishes average global refinement from worst local hidden revision burden.

**Forbidden inference**

```text
adequate for the original task
    =>
revisable for a later task
```

The synthesis should not conclude that every chapter was one theorem. It should conclude that the chapters inhabit one typed universe because each supplies a different condition governing whether a scientific representation can travel:

```text
Capability  : does epistemic burden remain bounded?
Future      : does closed compression survive opening?
History     : does inherited meaning survive replacement?
Mechanism   : does visible state support one law?
Evidence    : has the required distinction been earned?
Learning    : which uncertainty is the next observation reducing?
Loss        : is the representation faithful to the loss response?
Warning     : does temporal ordering discriminate and transport?
Revision    : can the old compression answer the revised task?
```

The common result is not one privileged ecological state. It is a set of explicit bridge, factorization, repair, evidence, and portability conditions for moving between task-indexed scientific worldlines.

## 3. Novelty firewalls for the dissertation

1. Do not title or frame the dissertation as a unified theory of ecological state alone.
2. Do not count purpose relativity, quotient construction, partition refinement, or fixed-task factorization as separate novelty contributions.
3. Give every research chapter one source-owned result and one primary forbidden inference.
4. Embed TU-2 in RACH, TU-3 in the loss chapter, and TU-4 in the warning chapter.
5. Keep TU-1 in the General Synthesis unless carrier-changing revision becomes an independently strong result.
6. Do not let `theouni` acquire ownership of source theorems or empirical evidence.
7. Do not convert pairwise non-conflict, open bridges, or two bounded triadic witnesses into a global consistency claim.
8. Do not claim a natural ecological state without the Reality-to-Theory admission bridge.
