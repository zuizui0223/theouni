# Theory Universe v0.1 — a contract-relative ecology of worlds, states, evidence, and warning

This directory defines the **theory-first core** of `theouni` before any empirical system, species, island syndrome, SDM workflow, or sensor platform is projected into it.

The aim is not to merge repositories. It is to define one mathematically coherent universe in which their theoretical objects can coexist without type collapse.

## 0. Constitutional statement

The programme starts from six commitments.

1. **Nature is not a state.** Nature is treated as a temporally extended ecological reality.
2. **A scientific world is a model object, not reality itself.** Mathematics acts on an admissible world universe, not directly on nature.
3. **A state is a contract-relative quotient of worlds.** State identity is defined by what may safely be forgotten for a declared scientific responsibility.
4. **Evidence does not create ontic distinctions.** It only determines which required distinctions are currently identified and which reports are licensed.
5. **Causal uncertainty remains set-valued until discriminating evidence exists.** A model winner is not manufactured merely because a single explanation is convenient.
6. **Warning is conditional on a loss-generating state.** A warning statistic has no universal status independent of the state and endpoint process in which its ordering is evaluated.

The compact programme question is:

> **What may science safely forget, and for which declared purpose?**

## 1. Reality, model worlds, and the representation bridge

Let

\[
\mathfrak R
\]

denote ecological reality. `Reality` is deliberately left outside the finite theorem domain.

Scientific mathematics works on an admissible model-world universe

\[
\Omega.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak R \neq \Omega.}
\]

A modelling / measurement bridge

\[
\Pi_V:\mathfrak R\dashrightarrow \mathcal P(\Omega)
\]

maps a real ecological situation under scientific context `V` to one or more compatible model worlds. The map is allowed to be partial and set-valued: incomplete measurement or structural model uncertainty need not pick one exact model world.

A theorem proved on `Omega` is therefore not automatically an empirical claim about `Reality`.

## 2. Temporally extended ecological worlds

A canonical world at time `t` is represented schematically as

\[
\omega_t=(h_{\le t},x_t,\theta_t,K_t),
\]

where:

- `h_{<=t}` is relevant ecological history;
- `x_t` is the present ecological configuration;
- `theta_t` is retained latent mechanism / interaction structure;
- `K_t` is the declared future-response law under contemplated actions, interactions, or interventions.

This is a schema, not a claim that all ecological models must literally use these four coordinates.

The crucial type distinction is

\[
\boxed{\text{Snapshot} \neq \text{World} \neq \text{State}.}
\]

A snapshot is one present projection of a world. Snapshot sufficiency is therefore a theorem/question, never a default assumption.

## 3. Observation and intervention context

Science does not directly observe `omega`.

An observation/intervention context `V` determines the accessible record map

\[
O_V:\Omega\to \mathcal Y_V
\]

or, with stochastic observation,

\[
y\sim O_V(\cdot\mid\omega).
\]

Observed quantities such as trait values, visit counts, genetic statistics, suitability scores, or network summaries are therefore initially **observation coordinates**. They acquire state relevance only if the scientific contract and adequacy tests require them.

## 4. Scientific contract

A scientific contract is

\[
\mathcal C=(\Gamma,\mathcal H,\Theta,\mathcal D;T),
\]

where:

- `Gamma` specifies future operations, interventions, or response obligations the state must survive;
- `H` specifies inherited meaning or history that must remain coherent;
- `Theta` specifies retained mechanism alternatives whose response differences may matter;
- `D` specifies observation, experiment, reliability, calibration, and evidence obligations;
- `T` specifies the requested report, forecast, decision, warning endpoint, or other target.

A contract is purpose-relative but not arbitrary. Once declared, dynamics, mechanism disagreement, historical incoherence, and evidence can refute an attempted world merge.

## 5. Required ecological state

For two worlds `omega` and `omega'`, define

\[
\omega\sim_{\mathcal C}\omega'
\]

when they are scientifically interchangeable for every responsibility declared in `C`.

The required state space is the quotient

\[
\boxed{
S_{\mathcal C}=\Omega/\sim_{\mathcal C}.
}
\]

The required state occupied by world `omega` is

\[
q_{\mathcal C}(\omega)=[\omega]_{\mathcal C}.
\]

On a finite common carrier, CREST supplies the least-information / unique coarsest adequate partition under declared closure requirements. This directory treats that result as the canonical finite state constructor while keeping `Reality -> Omega` external validity separate.

### Safe forgetting

A distinction between two worlds may be forgotten exactly when merging them does not invalidate the declared scientific work.

Thus state construction is equivalently the search for the **maximal lawful forgetting** compatible with the contract.

## 6. Three structural obstruction families

The same quotient framework supports three currently separate theorem families.

### 6.1 Future obstruction — CCOC

Changing or enlarging `Gamma` can expose distinctions erased by an older quotient.

\[
\omega\sim_{\mathcal C}\omega'
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad
\omega\sim_{\mathcal C'}\omega'
\]

when `C'` adds future grammar, connections, or interventions that distinguish their responses.

### 6.2 Historical / semantic obstruction — MLTR

When the world carrier itself changes under turnover, replacement, extinction, recolonisation, or rewiring,

\[
\Omega_t\rightsquigarrow\Omega_{t+1},
\]

an inherited macro-state need not transport exactly. Repair or history augmentation is required when the carried merge is no longer operationally coherent.

### 6.3 Mechanism obstruction — MRM

Two worlds may share a visible present configuration while retaining different mechanisms. A mechanism distinction is state-relevant exactly when retained mechanisms disagree on a response required by the contract.

Full mechanism identity is therefore not the state; only response-relevant mechanism distinctions must survive the quotient.

## 7. Ecological laws as quotient laws

An ecological law is represented as an effective law on an adequate quotient.

For declared action set `A`, a deterministic coarse law

\[
L:S_{\mathcal C}\times A\to S_{\mathcal C}
\]

is well-defined only if every pair of worlds merged into one required state agrees on the state-relevant response under every declared action.

Therefore a law can be valid under one contract and fail after future, history, mechanism, or evidence responsibilities change. This need not mean that the old law was false in its original domain; the old quotient may simply cease to be adequate.

## 8. Evidence classes are not ecological states

After record `y`, define the evidence-compatible world class

\[
E(y)=\{\omega\in\Omega:\omega\text{ is compatible with }y\text{ under }\mathcal D\}.
\]

This is an epistemic object.

\[
\boxed{
\text{EvidenceClass}\neq\text{RequiredState}.
}
\]

A required state can exist mathematically while current evidence intersects several required-state blocks.

This yields the canonical separation

\[
\boxed{
\text{required state}
\neq
\text{identified state}
\neq
\text{reportable target}.
}
\]

For target `T`, the sharp report set is

\[
R_T(y)=\{T(\omega):\omega\in E(y)\}.
\]

A deterministic target report is licensed exactly when `R_T(y)` is a singleton, even if full state identity remains unresolved.

CED owns the finite evidence/reportability theory for this layer.

## 9. Causal admissibility and learning

Let each declared causal programme `m` generate or support a subset of model worlds `Omega_m`.

After evidence `y`, define

\[
A(y)=\{m:\Omega_m\cap E(y)\neq\varnothing\}.
\]

`A(y)` is the admissible causal set. It is not a posterior winner and not an ontic state.

RACH owns inference on this layer: retain compatible causal programmes, characterize replaceability / degeneracy, and choose feasible observations expected to reduce the unresolved causal set.

A next observation acts schematically as

\[
(E_t,A_t)\xrightarrow{a_t,y_{t+1}}(E_{t+1},A_{t+1}).
\]

Scientific learning is therefore refinement of justified uncertainty, not compulsory collapse to one explanation.

## 10. Dynamics and state closure

Model worlds evolve according to a declared transition law

\[
\omega_{t+1}\sim K(\cdot\mid\omega_t,a_t).
\]

The required state is

\[
s_t=q_{\mathcal C}(\omega_t).
\]

A candidate state is future-sufficient for the declared target only when the required future response factors through the state. In a stochastic form, the relevant conditional law must satisfy the corresponding factorization / Markov property on the declared contract.

A complete simulator state can be sufficient under one explicit simulator closure without being proved minimal, natural, or empirically measurable. Conversely, a coarse empirical summary can fail because worlds sharing that summary have different future-response signatures.

Eco-genetic criticality currently supplies a bounded constructive example of this distinction.

## 11. Loss-generating state and conditional warning

Let `L` be a declared future loss target or loss process. Construct the corresponding scientific contract

\[
\mathcal C_L.
\]

The required quotient

\[
S_L=S_{\mathcal C_L}
\]

is called a **loss-generating state** when it is sufficient for the declared loss process within the model/domain under study.

A warning statistic `G` is not itself the state. Warning validity is a relation evaluated conditionally within a frozen loss-generating state, for example through an ordering such as

\[
\tau_G<\tau_L.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\text{Warning validity is state-conditional, not statistic-universal.}
}
\]

The eco-genetic warning extension owns the current condition-recovery and within-state warning programme. Portability across separately calibrated states remains a separate question.

## 12. One canonical worldline

The theory universe can be summarized as two coupled flows.

### Ontic / dynamical flow

```text
Reality R
  |
  | modelling / measurement bridge Pi_V
  v
Admissible model-world universe Omega
  |
  v
World omega_t ---- dynamics K ----> World omega_{t+1}
  |
  | scientific contract C
  v
RequiredState q_C(omega_t)
```

### Epistemic / learning flow

```text
World omega_t
  |
  | observation contract O_D
  v
Record y_t
  |
  v
EvidenceClass E_t
  |
  +----> licensed Report_T(E_t)
  |
  v
AdmissibleCausalSet A_t
  |
  | next-observation design
  v
new record -> refined E_{t+1}, A_{t+1}
```

Warning is attached only after a loss contract has fixed a loss-generating state.

## 13. Type firewall

The following identifications are forbidden unless an explicit theorem/bridge proves the required factorization.

| Forbidden collapse | Reason |
|---|---|
| `Reality = ModelWorld` | model-world theorems do not automatically establish empirical truth |
| `Snapshot = World` | history, latent mechanism, and future-response structure may matter |
| `Snapshot = RequiredState` | snapshot sufficiency must be demonstrated |
| `CompleteSimulatorState = MinimalState` | simulator closure proves sufficiency, not minimality |
| `CompleteSimulatorState = NaturalState` | model representation and empirical ontology differ |
| `EvidenceClass = RequiredState` | evidence resolution and ontic adequacy are separate |
| `Mechanism = RequiredState` | only response-relevant mechanism differences must be retained |
| `Proxy = StateCoordinate` | calibration or held-out predictive adequacy is required |
| `Target = RequiredState` | a target can remain reportable while full state is unresolved |
| `WarningStatistic = WarningState` | warning is a relation within a loss-generating state |
| `HistoricalLabel = CurrentOperationalState` | transport / repair may be required after replacement |
| `ExactWorldIdentity = BestPredictionState` | maximal detail need not be target-adequate or efficient |
| `CausalWinner = AdmissibleCausalSet` | unresolved causal multiplicity must be retained |

## 15. Open mathematical obligations

Theory Universe v0.1 is intentionally not presented as closed. The main unresolved obligations are:

1. define the weakest conditions under which the `Reality -> Omega` projection earns empirical adequacy rather than mere model compatibility;
2. extend finite exact CREST state construction to useful stochastic / continuous / approximate settings without collapsing model state and natural state;
3. formalize composition rules among Future, History, Mechanism, and Evidence contracts and determine when their refinement operators commute or fail to commute;
4. prove conditions under which RACH next-observation reduction and CED licensed-resolution gain coincide or diverge;
5. characterize when a loss-generating quotient is invariant under changes of model representation;
6. define portability of warning across different loss-generating states without assuming a universal threshold;
7. formalize empirical state discovery as an out-of-sample factorization problem rather than proxy accumulation.

These are research questions, not already established claims.

## 16. Boundary to the empirical universe

No species, island syndrome, floral polymorphism, SDM method, camera system, or field protocol is part of Theory Universe v0.1.

They enter later through explicit projections of the form

\[
\text{empirical system}
\to
(\Omega,\mathcal C,O_{\mathcal D},T)
\]

with a stated claim ceiling.

That later layer may contain thistles, island syndromes, floral trait construction, SDM-derived field design, visit cameras, and other concrete research programmes. Their role will be to instantiate, test, falsify, or expose missing coordinates in the theory universe—not to define the core types by example.
